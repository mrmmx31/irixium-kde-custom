IrixClassic-SGI 0.4.1

Original SVG geometry and native-size PNG renderings generated by
build_classic.py, classic_extensions.py and the classic identity artwork modules. MIT license; see LICENSE.
953 canonical icons; 481 detailed compositions; 2380 supported names.

Inspired by SGI Indigo Magic User Interface Guidelines, chapter 2:
https://techpubs.jurassic.nl/library/manuals/2000/007-2167-002/sgi_html/ch02.html
Independent adaptation, not extracted from IRIX or an official SGI product.
No third-party icon artwork or font files are included.

Audit-derived coverage: icons/sources/classic-audit-coverage.json.
MIME candidates do not prove runtime use. Application aliases represent
reviewed names. Application identities are independently drawn from their original motifs. Symbolic actions are monochrome; tray and launcher retain SGI colours.
No external audit application is included. Other names inherit breeze,hicolor.
